# Forestry

Potential workflows: plantation inventory, stand boundary mapping, canopy/cover monitoring, disturbance/change detection, fire/storm impact assessment and operational planning.

Document species and age if known, planting/management history, stand density, terrain, sensor/date, pixel size, seasonality and field validation. Distinguish tree-cover detection from species identification, and canopy signals from timber volume or resin yield.

Satellite indicators can prioritize inspection; they do not replace plot sampling or direct measurement.
