# Forestry and forest monitoring

## Scope
Forest plantations, native vegetation, agroforestry, stand condition, canopy cover, disturbance, fire impacts, land-cover change and plantation management context.

## Required context
Identify species or forest type if known, stand age, planting/harvest history, management objective, location, observation date and spatial unit. Do not infer exact species or age from imagery alone unless the evidence supports it.

## Remote sensing
Use appropriate optical or radar data for the question. Optical indices can indicate spectral/vegetation differences; radar backscatter responds to structure, moisture, geometry and acquisition conditions. Neither is a direct biomass, timber volume, resin yield or tree-health measurement without calibration.

## Field interpretation
Distinguish canopy gaps, bare soil, shadows, harvest blocks, roads, fire scars and seasonal changes where image resolution allows. Confirm ambiguous findings with field observations and suitable temporal imagery.

## Production claims
Do not infer timber volume, growth, resin production or economic yield from a single image or generic coefficient. Require plot measurements, species, age, density, management and a validated model.
