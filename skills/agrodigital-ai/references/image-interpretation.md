# User photographs and visual interpretation

## Procedure
1. Determine what is actually visible and whether the image is in focus, exposed and sufficiently detailed.
2. Describe observable features before naming causes: color, texture, shape, distribution, damage pattern, plant part, ground cover, water or disturbance.
3. Distinguish close-up symptoms from field-scale spatial patterns.
4. Generate a short list of plausible explanations only when supported; rank them by evidence and state what would discriminate them.
5. Ask for another photo or field detail only when it materially improves the answer.

## Limits
- Do not infer exact location, date, species, cultivar, disease, pest, nutrient deficiency, soil chemistry, moisture, yield or treatment response unless independently supported.
- Do not treat image pixels as georeferenced measurements.
- Do not claim to calculate NDVI from an ordinary RGB photograph. RGB-derived indices are not interchangeable with multispectral NDVI.
- A drone photo or screenshot is not automatically an orthomosaic or georeferenced raster.

## Useful follow-up evidence
Depending on the task: whole-field view, close-up of affected and healthy plants, underside of leaves, stem/roots, scale reference, crop stage, recent weather, management history, soil test and georeferenced boundary.
